Page Watch — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 14, 2026 · Version 1.0

TL;DR: Page Watch runs 100% inside your browser. Your monitors, snapshots, and change history never leave your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and nothing to sell.

1. What Page Watch does

Page Watch is a Chrome extension that periodically revisits web pages you choose to monitor, compares them against locally stored snapshots, and notifies you of changes. All monitoring, storage, and comparison happen on your own device.

2. Data we collect: none

We operate no backend servers. Page Watch does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data about you anywhere outside your own browser. Specifically:

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up; nothing is tied to an identity we hold.
  • No analytics or telemetry. We do not track usage, installs, or behavior.
  • No third-party trackers. The extension loads no ads, SDKs, or tracking scripts.

3. Data stored locally on your device

The following data is stored only in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and is never sent to us:

  • The URLs, selectors, and labels of monitors you create
  • Text snapshots and small pixel signatures of the monitored regions, used for change detection
  • Change history (up to 50 events per monitor)
  • Your preferences (check interval, notification setting, ignore rules)
  • Your license key and its verification status (see §5)

Deleting the extension (or its data via chrome://extensions) permanently removes all of this.

4. Network requests the extension makes

  • To pages you monitor. Page Watch fetches only the URLs you explicitly add, at the interval you set, from your own machine. These requests go directly from your browser to that website — they never pass through us. Please respect the target site's terms of service and use reasonable check intervals.
  • To the license server (Pro only). If you enter a license key, the extension verifies it with the license provider (Gumroad). This request contains only your license key and the product identifier. Verification results are cached locally for 7 days so the extension keeps working offline.

5. License key (Pro version)

Pro unlocks are handled through Gumroad. When you activate a license, your key is sent to Gumroad's license verification API for validation. Purchase records (email, payment) are held by Gumroad under Gumroad's privacy policy — not by us. We receive no purchase data and cannot see your payment information.

6. Permissions and why we need them

  • alarms — schedule periodic checks without keeping a process running
  • storage — keep your monitors and history on-device
  • notifications — alert you when a monitored page changes
  • tabs / scripting — capture the monitored region for visual diff, and run the element picker on the page you are viewing
  • offscreen — parse fetched HTML and compute image signatures
  • Host access (<all_urls>) — needed because you choose which sites to monitor; the extension only ever accesses pages you add or the tab you pick an element from

7. Children

Page Watch is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
